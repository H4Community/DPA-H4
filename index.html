Teacher & Principal Corner

Curriculum-Ready & Board-Safe

  • Ontario 20-Minute Daily DPA Fit: Each 5-minute random groove session delivers moderate-to-vigorous rhythmic movement that counts directly toward Ontario Daily Physical Activity (DPA) goals across all divisions.
  • 100% Desk-Side Safe: All 10 foundational hip-hop grooves (Bounce, Rock, Hop, Slide, Shift, Shake, Twist, Tick, Roll, Wave) stay within arm's reach beside student desks—no gym booking required.
  • Zero Student Data or Logins: Runs locally in the browser with zero cookies, zero student accounts, zero camera access, and zero external video ads.
